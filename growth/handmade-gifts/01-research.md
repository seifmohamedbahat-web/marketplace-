# Research brief: Handmade Gifts — Global Market

## Demand signals

- The global personalized-gifts market is estimated at ~$34B in 2026, growing
  at a 6.7% CAGR; the handmade-premium-gifts segment specifically is growing
  faster (~11.5%), reflecting demand outpacing mass personalization.
  ([Business Research Insights](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/personalized-gifts-market-102185))
- Buyers are moving away from overconsumption toward fewer, higher-meaning
  purchases — quality and story matter more than price. Over 65% of
  consumers say they prefer personalized items.
  ([Craft Industry Alliance](https://craftindustryalliance.org/2026-retail-product-trend-report/))
- Top-selling handmade sub-categories right now: nameplate/birthstone
  jewelry, crochet & embroidered textiles (Gen Z anti-fast-fashion pull),
  and wellness-adjacent handmade goods (aromatherapy items, etc.).
  ([Afoma Marketplace](https://afomamarketplace.com/blogs/handmade-jewelry-trends-in-2026-what-customers-are-actually-buying))

## Competitor angles

- Etsy still has ~86.5M active buyers and dominant SEO/traffic, but sellers
  increasingly cite rising fees, algorithm dependence, and unpredictable
  policy changes as reasons to diversify.
  ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/alternatives-to-selling-on-etsy))
- The winning seller pattern in 2026 is multi-channel: keep Etsy for
  discovery, add Shopify for brand/customer ownership, plus one more
  marketplace for additional discovery — most successful handmade
  businesses run 3-4 channels at once.
  ([mysellerhub](https://mysellerhub.com/blog/top-10-etsy-alternatives-for-sellers-in-2026/))
- This is Global Market's opening: sellers aren't looking to *replace* Etsy,
  they're actively looking for the "one more channel" to add.

## Seller acquisition hooks

1. Position as the additional discovery channel handmade sellers are already
   adding in 2026 — not an Etsy-replacement pitch, an Etsy-plus pitch.
2. Global Market's category page is curated (six categories, no product
   noise) vs. Etsy's algorithm-driven feed — easier for a new seller's work
   to actually be seen.
3. "Custom product creators" framing in Global Market's own positioning
   already matches exactly what's converting right now: personalization,
   not mass-produced goods.
4. Verified-seller spotlight + trust badges (already on the homepage) speak
   directly to buyers' 2026 shift toward valuing authenticity/craftsmanship
   provenance.

## Content angles

1. "Why 2026 is the year of the extra channel" — seller-facing piece on the
   multi-channel trend.
2. "Personalization over production" — buyer-facing piece tying Global
   Market's custom-creator model to the anti-overconsumption trend.
3. Category spotlight on nameplate/birthstone jewelry and
   crochet/embroidery as the two fastest-moving sub-trends.
4. "One-of-one, not one-of-a-thousand" — brand line contrasting handmade
   authenticity with mass personalization at big-box retailers.
