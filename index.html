01 — About

We treat concrete & steel like they matter.

Metro Wash Pros is a professional pressure washing crew built for people who notice detail.

We run hot-water pressure washing and soft-wash equipment calibrated for the surface in front of us — never a one-setting-fits-all hose job. Every technician is trained on surface chemistry, pressure limits, and runoff control before they touch a client's property.

We show up on schedule, protect landscaping and fixtures, and leave the site cleaner than the surface we were hired to wash. If a job doesn't meet standard, we return and make it right at no cost.

Backed by a written guarantee

If you're not satisfied with the result, we re-clean the affected area free of charge — no fine print.
